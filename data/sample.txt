Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. All remote work must be
approved in advance by a direct manager. Remote employees are required to be
available online between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone.

Acme Corp provides a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars to each remote
employee. This stipend can be used for a desk, chair, or monitor. The stipend
must be claimed within the first 90 days of employment.

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before any remote work
begins. Employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal
systems from outside the office.
